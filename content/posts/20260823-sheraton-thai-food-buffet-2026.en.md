<p>Grand Café at <strong>Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel</strong> in Maihama serves a <strong>Thai Food Buffet</strong> from Tuesday, September 1 to Saturday, October 31, 2026.</p>

<p>The fair is supervised by Attapol "Naito" Tantong (Chef X), executive chef of Bangkok Marriott Hotel The Surawongse. Rotating daily dishes include crab curry stir-fry (poo pad pong curry), khao soi gai, tom yum kung and green curry.</p>

<h2>Collaboration sweets with THANN</h2>

<p>The buffet also features a collaboration with <strong>THANN</strong>, the Thai natural skincare brand. Sweets inspired by three of its signature scents appear in stages:</p>

<ul>
<li>Sept 1 – 20: Savarin</li>
<li>Sept 21 – Oct 11: Lime jelly</li>
<li>Oct 12 – 31: Jasmine brûlée</li>
</ul>

<h2>From the Hawaiian buffet to an autumn Thai fair</h2>

<p>Grand Café runs its Hawaiian buffet through August 31, then switches to the Thai fair from September 1 — the restaurant changes its theme seasonally, so it is a good pick if you enjoy hotel buffets with a twist during your park trip.</p>

<p>Hours: lunch 11:30–14:30 (until 15:00 on weekends and holidays), dinner 17:00–22:30. On weekends and holidays, seatings are limited to 90 minutes for lunch and 120 minutes for dinner.</p>

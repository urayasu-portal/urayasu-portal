<p>Tokyo DisneySea's ride <strong>Indiana Jones Adventure: Temple of the Crystal Skull</strong> reopens on Monday, November 30, 2026, following a long refurbishment and inspection period.</p>

<p>According to the announcement by Oriental Land Co., the refurbishment adds projection-mapping effects and new audio, making the experience more immersive than before.</p>

<h2>What's new</h2>

<p>Indiana Jones Adventure is a high-speed dark ride in the Lost River Delta area of Tokyo DisneySea, racing through an ancient temple in a jungle expedition vehicle. The ride lasts about 3 minutes, seats 12 per vehicle, and requires a height of 117 cm or more.</p>

<p>The officially announced changes are the addition of projection-mapping effects and new audio.</p>

<h2>Reopening on November 30</h2>

<p>Operating hours and how the ride will be offered after reopening (standby, Priority Pass or Premier Access) have not been announced yet. If your trip is planned around the reopening date, check the official Tokyo Disney Resort website and app closer to your visit.</p>

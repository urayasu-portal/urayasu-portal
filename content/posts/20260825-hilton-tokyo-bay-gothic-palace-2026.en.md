<p>Lounge O at <strong>Hilton Tokyo Bay</strong> — one of the official hotels next to Tokyo Disney Resort — holds a Halloween sweets buffet called <strong>"Gothic Palace"</strong> from September 5 to November 3, 2026.</p>

<p>It runs on weekends and public holidays only during the period, with about 20 kinds of sweets and 15 savory dishes in a 2-hour seating.</p>

<h2>Autumn sweets in a gothic palace setting</h2>

<p>At the live station, chefs finish a "Marron Chocolat Mont Blanc" — layers of chocolate ice cream and chestnut cream — in front of you.</p>

<p>The line-up also features autumn ingredients such as chestnut and purple sweet potato, plus Halloween-themed decorations. With around 15 savory dishes as well, it works as a lunch, not just dessert.</p>

<p>Prices: adults ¥5,000, ages 4–8 ¥1,900, ages 9–12 ¥2,700. Four seatings per day: 12:00, 12:30, 14:30 and 15:00.</p>

<h2>One of several autumn options at the hotel</h2>

<p>A photo booth matching the theme is set up near the venue entrance. Hilton Tokyo Bay also starts an autumn afternoon tea at a separate venue from September 1 — the buffet suits a lively weekend visit with family or friends, while the afternoon tea suits a slower afternoon.</p>

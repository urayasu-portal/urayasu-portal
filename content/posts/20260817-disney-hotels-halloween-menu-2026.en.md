<p>Three Disney hotels in the Maihama area serve <strong>Disney Halloween special menus</strong> from September 15 to October 31, 2026.</p>

<p>The line-up covers Disney Ambassador Hotel, Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta and Tokyo Disneyland Hotel, each offering different buffets, course meals and seasonal drinks.</p>

<h2>What each hotel serves</h2>

<p>At <strong>Disney Ambassador Hotel</strong>, the Chef Mickey lunch buffet is ¥7,200 on weekdays and ¥7,500 on weekends and holidays for adults. Empire Grill offers a signature dinner at ¥14,300, among other menus.</p>

<p>At <strong>Hotel MiraCosta</strong>, the Oceano buffet is ¥7,500 on weekdays and ¥8,000 on weekends and holidays for adults. Besides a dinner course, Silk Road Garden and BellaVista Lounge also serve limited-time menus.</p>

<p>At <strong>Tokyo Disneyland Hotel</strong>, the lunchtime buffet at Sherwood Garden Restaurant is ¥6,800 on weekdays and ¥7,300 on weekends and holidays for adults. Halloween menus are also available at Canna and Dreamers Lounge.</p>

{{< warn >}}Menu contents, prices, serving hours and reservation availability are subject to change. Check each hotel's official page before you go.{{< /warn >}}

<h2>Picking a restaurant for your Halloween trip</h2>

<p>The three hotels differ in style — buffet, course dining or casual sets — so you can choose by budget and occasion. Reservations for Disney hotel restaurants can fill up quickly during the Halloween season, so booking early is recommended.</p>

<p>Tokyo Disney Resort's paid line-skip service, <strong>Disney Premier Access (DPA)</strong>, changes its pricing on September 1, 2026.</p>

<p>Four attractions are affected — one at Tokyo Disneyland and three at Tokyo DisneySea — and each goes up by ¥500 per ride.</p>

<h2>New prices for Beauty and the Beast, Frozen Journey, Soaring and more</h2>

<table>
<tr><th>Park</th><th>Attraction</th><th>Until Aug 31</th><th>From Sept 1</th></tr>
<tr><td>Tokyo Disneyland</td><td>Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast</td><td>¥2,000</td><td>¥2,500</td></tr>
<tr><td>Tokyo DisneySea</td><td>Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey</td><td>¥2,000</td><td>¥2,500</td></tr>
<tr><td>Tokyo DisneySea</td><td>Soaring: Fantastic Flight</td><td>¥2,000</td><td>¥2,500</td></tr>
<tr><td>Tokyo DisneySea</td><td>Journey to the Center of the Earth</td><td>¥1,500</td><td>¥2,000</td></tr>
</table>

<p>DPA is purchased in the official app on the day of your visit, separately from your park ticket, and lets you ride the selected attraction at a reserved time slot.</p>

<h2>Four more attractions join DPA on September 1</h2>

<p>On the same day, the list of DPA-eligible attractions grows.</p>

<p>At Tokyo Disneyland, <strong>Big Thunder Mountain</strong>, <strong>Pooh's Hunny Hunt</strong> and <strong>Monsters, Inc. "Ride &amp; Go Seek!"</strong> are added; at Tokyo DisneySea, <strong>Raging Spirits</strong> joins the service.</p>

<p>The free 40th Anniversary Priority Pass ends on August 31, so the mix of free and paid line-skip options changes significantly from September. If you are visiting in autumn 2026, it is worth deciding in advance which attractions are worth the extra cost for your group.</p>

{{< warn >}}Disney Premier Access is sold in limited numbers and may be unavailable depending on the day's park operations. Check the official Tokyo Disney Resort website and app for the latest eligible attractions and prices.{{< /warn >}}

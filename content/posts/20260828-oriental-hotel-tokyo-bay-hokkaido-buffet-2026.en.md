<p>Chinese Table, the restaurant at <strong>Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay</strong> — directly connected to Shin-Urayasu Station, two stops from Maihama — holds the buffet fair <strong>"Hokkaido Taste Journey"</strong> from Tuesday, September 1 to Tuesday, November 3, 2026.</p>

<p>The highlight is a comparison of two kinds of crab — snow crab and red snow crab — alongside Chinese dishes built around Hokkaido ingredients.</p>

<h2>Crab, seafood and Hokkaido flavors</h2>

<p>The buffet features dishes such as Genghis Khan (grilled lamb) spare ribs, seared scallop with smoked salmon, steamed sockeye salmon with vegetables, and zangi fried chicken.</p>

<p>Seafood ramen, dim sum and annin tofu with Hokkaido fruit sauce round out the line-up — Hokkaido flavors served Chinese-style. A tea buffet with about 20 varieties is also available.</p>

<h2>No time limit, right at Shin-Urayasu Station</h2>

<p>On both weekdays and weekends, you can stay as long as the restaurant is open — there is no time limit.</p>

<p>Because the hotel sits directly above Shin-Urayasu Station, it is an easy buffet option without going into the Maihama resort area — convenient for a rest-day lunch or dinner during a Disney trip.</p>

Shibuya is the Tokyo your teenagers have seen in a hundred videos — the **Scramble Crossing** and the open-air rooftop of **SHIBUYA SKY** 229 metres above it. It is also, for a family staying near Tokyo Disney Resort, one of the easier central-Tokyo trips: **about 40 minutes and ¥252 from Urayasu Station with one subway transfer**, arriving directly under the building. The difficulty is not the journey but the ticket: SHIBUYA SKY uses **dated time slots that go on sale exactly two weeks ahead**, and sunset is gone within minutes. This guide covers the 2026 price rules, the strict rooftop rules (no strollers, no bags), the alternatives if you miss the ticket, and a half-day that leaves energy for the evening.

## Who is this for?

- Teens and adults who want **the** Tokyo rooftop photo — the open-air corner looking down on the crossing.
- A family with older kids doing a "modern Tokyo" day; the crossing, Hachiko and Miyashita Park are free and quick.
- Travelers comparing it with [Tokyo Skytree](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-skytree/): SKY is lower but open-air and far more photogenic; Skytree is enclosed, has a direct bus from the parks and is cheaper for small children.
- Not ideal for: toddlers in strollers, rainy days, or anyone unwilling to plan two weeks ahead.

{{< note >}}
**The 2026 price structure in one line:** price depends on **entry time (before or after 15:00)** and **where you buy (online is ¥300 cheaper)** — not on the day of the week. Adults are 12 and over; children 11 and under pay ¥1,200 at the counter only; under 6 free.

## What to expect

### SHIBUYA SKY (14F ticket gate, 46F indoor floor, rooftop)

The ticket gate is on the **14th floor of Shibuya Scramble Square** (the tower directly above Shibuya Station). You rise to the **46th-floor indoor gallery** (shop, café, lockers, nursing room, the only place tripods are allowed) and then step out onto the **open-air rooftop**: a helipad-style deck with glass-edged corners, a hammock-net area and a 360-degree view from Skytree to, on a clear day, Mount Fuji.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hours** | 10:00–22:30, last entry 21:20 (ticket counter 10:00–21:20); closed January 1; ad-hoc closures posted online |
| **Adults (12+)** | Entry before 15:00: **online ¥2,700 / counter ¥3,000** · Entry 15:00 or later: **online ¥3,400 / counter ¥3,700** |
| **Children (11 and under)** | **¥1,200, counter only**, must be with a ticketed adult (sold even when adult tickets are sold out) |
| **Under 6** | Free |
| **Sales** | Online from **00:00 JST, 14 days before** the entry date; time-slot tickets; late arrival may be refused; no re-entry; no time limit once inside |
| **Rooftop rules** | Only a phone, pocket camera or binoculars allowed on the roof; bags, hats, tripods, selfie sticks, food and drink go into free 46F lockers; **personal strollers not allowed on the roof** (limited loaner strollers at 46F information; carrier recommended) |
| **Weather** | The roof closes without notice in rain, strong wind, lightning or extreme heat — the indoor 46F floor stays open |

**Which slot?** The 15:00+ price band exists because sunset is the prize. If sunset is sold out, the **first morning slot (10:00)** is cheaper, clearer and uncrowded — and the crossing is just as mesmerising in daylight. For a toddler, the morning slot plus a carrier is the only sane version.

### The Scramble Crossing and cheaper views

Crossing it is free and takes 30 seconds; the fun is watching it. Options, cheapest first:

- **Street level and the station walkways** — free, chaotic, fine for a quick photo.
- **CROSSING VIEW & ROOFTOP LOUNGE MAG8** on top of **MAGNET by SHIBUYA109**, the building on the corner of the crossing: **¥1,800 with one drink, 10:00–22:00 (last entry 21:30)**, may close in bad weather. The closest overhead angle of the crossing itself (SHIBUYA SKY is higher but further away).
- **SHIBUYA SKY** — the full skyline.

### Hachiko, Miyashita Park and the rest

- **Hachiko statue** at the JR Hachiko exit — free, a two-minute photo stop; the line for a photo is itself a Shibuya experience.
- **Miyashita Park** — a rooftop park over a mall two minutes north: **park 8:00–23:00**, with a skate park, bouldering wall and sand court (paid), and **RAYARD Miyashita Park** below it (shops 11:00–21:00, food hall 11:00–22:30, weekends from 10:30). The food hall is the family lunch answer.
- **Shopping:** Shibuya 109 (teen fashion), the Parco and Hikarie basements, Loft and Tokyu Hands for Japanese stationery and gadgets — all within 10 minutes on foot. **Harajuku** (Takeshita Street, Meiji Shrine) is one JR stop or a 15-minute walk north.

## How to get there from the Tokyo Disney Resort area

**From Urayasu Station (Tozai Line) — recommended:**

- **Option A:** Tozai Line to **Kudanshita**, **Hanzomon Line** to **Shibuya** — the Hanzomon platforms are directly beneath Shibuya Scramble Square; follow signs for the Scramble Square / SHIBUYA SKY elevators. About **40 minutes, IC ¥252**.
- **Option B:** Tozai Line to **Nihombashi**, **Ginza Line** to **Shibuya** (terminus, arrives on the 3rd floor of the station complex, by Hachiko). About 38 minutes, ¥252.

**From Maihama (Keiyo Line):** Keiyo Line to **Shin-Kiba**, Yurakucho Line to **Nagatacho**, Hanzomon Line to **Shibuya** — about **45 minutes, ¥451**, two transfers underground. The Tokyo Station + Yamanote Line route (about 55 minutes, ¥440) works but adds the long Keiyo-platform walk; see [Day trips into Tokyo](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-sightseeing-access/).

**From Shin-Urayasu:** add one Keiyo Line stop, or take the bus to Urayasu Station for the Tozai route.

Suica/PASMO works on every route; contactless credit cards work on Tokyo Metro but not on the JR Keiyo Line.

## A half-day plan (morning slot, lunch, home by 15:00)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:45 | Leave Urayasu Station; arrive Shibuya about 9:30 |
| 9:30 | Hachiko photo; cross the Scramble Crossing once while it is still half-empty |
| 10:00 | SHIBUYA SKY (first slot booked two weeks ago) — 60–90 minutes |
| 11:45 | MAG8 drink over the crossing (optional), or Loft/Hands for souvenirs |
| 12:30 | Lunch at the Miyashita Park food hall |
| 14:00 | Hanzomon or Ginza Line back; rest at the hotel before an evening in Ikspiari or the parks |

For a sunset version, book the 15:00+ slot nearest sunset, eat early, and expect to be back in Urayasu around 20:00.

## With kids, and when it rains

- **Strollers:** the building is fully accessible, but the **roof is not** for personal strollers. Loaner strollers exist but are limited; a carrier is the practical answer for under-3s. Everything else (bags, snacks, hats) goes in lockers.
- **Restrooms and nursing:** 46F has a nursing room; restrooms are inside the indoor floor.
- **Rain or wind:** the rooftop closes; you can still visit the 46F indoor floor, but the ticket is for the roof. Check the weather the day before, and swap a rainy-day visit for [Tokyo Character Street](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-character-street/) or [SMALL WORLDS](/en/travel-guide/small-worlds/).
- **Crowds:** Shibuya at night on weekends is a crush; with younger kids keep it to the morning.

## Pair it with

- **Harajuku and Meiji Shrine** — 15 minutes on foot north; shrine forest in the morning, Takeshita Street snacks, then Shibuya for the afternoon.
- [Tokyo Character Street](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-character-street/) — if character shopping is the real goal, Tokyo Station is on the way home.
- [Tokyo Skytree](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-skytree/) — the other tower; most families pick one.

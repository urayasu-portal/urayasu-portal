Stand on the Maihama Station platform and look west: the bay you see is the same bay that wraps around **Odaiba**. It is the big Tokyo playground that is genuinely close to Tokyo Disney Resort — **about 30 minutes door to door, with one simple transfer at Shin-Kiba** and no Tokyo Station maze. In 2026 it also gained the thing it was missing: a free, no-reservation **fountain show on the water, TOKYO AQUA SYMPHONY**, which runs from mid-afternoon into the night. That makes Odaiba a near-perfect "slow start, finish with lights" rest day for a family that spent yesterday in a park.

## Who is this for?

- You want a **rest day that starts late** — Odaiba's best moments are from 15:00 onward.
- You are traveling with kids who still need something to look at, but not another full day of queues.
- You want **Rainbow Bridge and the Tokyo skyline** in your photos without paying for an observation deck.
- You'd like a mall day with a sea breeze (the three malls are 5 minutes apart on foot).

{{< note >}}
**What changed in Odaiba recently.** The former Palette Town complex (VenusFort, the giant Ferris wheel, teamLab Borderless' original home) closed in 2022; **TOYOTA ARENA TOKYO** opened on part of that site in October 2025. The **life-size Unicorn Gundam statue ended its exhibition on August 31, 2026**, and **Madame Tussauds Tokyo closed on September 30, 2026**. Older guides still list all of these — this page doesn't.
{{< /note >}}

## What to do

### TOKYO AQUA SYMPHONY — the free fountain show (from 15:00)

Opened on March 28, 2026 at Odaiba Marine Park, this is a large water-and-light show in the bay with the Rainbow Bridge as its backdrop. **It is free and needs no reservation.** Since July 4, 2026 the regular timetable is **10 shows a day: 15:00, 15:45, 16:30, 17:15, 18:00, 18:45, 19:30, 20:15, 21:00 and 21:30**; programs rotate (game and anime music among them), and the night shows add lighting. Shows are suspended for maintenance days, events and bad weather — in October 2026, for example, several afternoon slots and a three-day full suspension (October 26–28) were announced. **Check the official schedule page the morning you go.**

Daylight shows are easier with small children (you can actually see the water); the 18:45–20:15 slots give you sunset, the bridge lights and a reasonable bedtime.

### Odaiba Marine Park: a beach, the Statue of Liberty replica and the bridge view

A metropolitan waterfront park that is open year-round with no gate. There is a real sandy beach (**swimming is not allowed**), a promenade, and the classic photo angle of the Rainbow Bridge with the small Statue of Liberty replica in front. Toddlers will happily dig in the sand for an hour; teens will take the skyline photos. It sits between the malls and the fountain, so it works as a short stop rather than a destination.

### Walk the Rainbow Bridge (free, 1.7 km)

A pedestrian promenade runs along the bridge from the Odaiba side to Shibaura. **April–October 9:00–21:00, November–March 10:00–18:00**, last entry 30 minutes before closing, **closed on the third Monday** (or the next day when that is a holiday). It takes about 30–40 minutes one way at a stroll, with the bay on both sides — spectacular, windy, and not a great idea for a child who is already tired. Bicycles must be pushed, pets must be inside a carrier, and it closes in strong wind. Do it as an out-and-back from the Odaiba side if you don't want to end up on the far shore.

### The malls: which one for what

| Mall | Hours (shops) | Why go |
|---|---|---|
| **DiverCity Tokyo Plaza** | Weekdays 11:00–20:00 / weekends & holidays 10:00–21:00 (food court to 21:00 / 22:00) | Biggest food court; THE GUNDAM BASE TOKYO shop (the outdoor statue is gone) |
| **Aqua City Odaiba** | Weekdays 11:00–20:00 / weekends & holidays 11:00–21:00 (restaurants to 23:00) | Bay-view restaurants facing the Rainbow Bridge; cinema |
| **DECKS Tokyo Beach** | Weekdays 11:00–20:00 / weekends & holidays 11:00–21:00 (restaurants to 23:00) | **LEGOLAND Discovery Center Tokyo** (indoor, tickets from ¥2,350 online) and **Tokyo Joypolis** (indoor arcade and rides: entry ¥1,700 adult / ¥1,400 elementary to high-school students, all-rides passport ¥6,000 / ¥5,000) |

If rain arrives, DECKS is the fallback — LEGOLAND Discovery Center is a solid 2–3 hours for ages roughly 3–10. (The Takoyaki Museum inside DECKS is closed from October 26 to December 4, 2026.)

### Fuji TV "Hachitama" observation sphere

The silver sphere on the Fuji TV building is a 25th-floor observation room: **10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), adults ¥800, elementary and junior-high students ¥500, preschoolers free, closed Mondays** (and on some other days — October 2026 had irregular closures, so check the visitor page). It is the cheapest high view in the area and a 15-minute activity, so it slots in easily before the fountain.

## How to get there from the Tokyo Disney Resort area

**From Maihama (recommended):**

1. **JR Keiyo Line** from Maihama, two stops toward Tokyo to **Shin-Kiba** — about 6 minutes, IC ¥199.
2. Change to the **Rinkai Line** (same station complex, follow the signs — a few minutes' walk) and ride to **Tokyo Teleport** — about 7 minutes, **IC ¥272**. Tokyo Teleport is the Rinkai Line's own station in the middle of Odaiba.
3. From Tokyo Teleport: DiverCity is right there; the fountain and the beach are about 10 minutes on foot.

That's roughly **30 minutes and ¥471 one way**, and you never touch Tokyo Station. From Shin-Urayasu, add one stop (about 3 minutes) on the Keiyo Line.

**From Urayasu Station (Tozai Line):** ride to Nihombashi, change to the Ginza Line for Shimbashi, then the **Yurikamome** to Daiba or Odaiba-Kaihinkoen (IC ¥252 + ¥325). It works, but it is a longer trip with two changes — if you can start from Maihama or Shin-Urayasu, use the Keiyo + Rinkai route above.

**Inside Odaiba**, the elevated **Yurikamome** line hops between Odaiba-Kaihinkoen, Daiba, Aomi (the arena) and on to Toyosu. Fares are ¥189–388 per ride (IC); a one-day pass is ¥820. Most people walk — the whole core is a 15-minute triangle.

{{< warn >}}
**Payment:** your Suica/PASMO works on every train in this route. Tapping a contactless credit card now works on Tokyo Metro, Toei and the Yurikamome, but **not on the JR Keiyo Line from Maihama and not on the Rinkai Line** — so keep a Suica or PASMO for this trip. See our [IC card guide](/en/travel-guide/ic-card-payment/).

## A half-day plan that ends with the lights

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 13:30 | Leave Maihama after a slow morning; arrive Tokyo Teleport around 14:00 |
| 14:00–15:00 | Late lunch at the DiverCity food court |
| 15:00 or 15:45 | First fountain show (daylight version) from the Odaiba Marine Park waterfront |
| 15:30–17:00 | Beach and promenade, or Hachitama (until 18:00), or LEGOLAND Discovery Center for younger kids |
| 17:30–18:30 | Early dinner at Aqua City with the bridge view |
| 18:45 or 19:30 | Evening fountain show with lights, then back via Shin-Kiba to Maihama by 21:00 |

For a full day, add the Rainbow Bridge walk in the morning (before the wind picks up) — but on a true rest day, less is more.

## With kids, and when it rains

- **Strollers** are fine everywhere: the malls, the park promenade and the Rinkai Line stations have elevators. The beach itself is sand.
- **Restrooms and nursing rooms** are in all three malls; the park has public toilets.
- **Wind** is the real weather variable in Odaiba. It can cancel the fountain, close the bridge promenade and make the beach unpleasant even on a sunny day. On a rainy or very windy forecast, swap to [teamLab Planets](/en/travel-guide/teamlab-planets/), [SMALL WORLDS](/en/travel-guide/small-worlds/) or [Tokyo Character Street](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-character-street/) instead of forcing it.

## Pair it with

- **teamLab Planets and Toyosu** are on the same side of the bay: Toyosu is one Yurakucho Line stop from Shin-Kiba, and the Yurikamome links Toyosu to Odaiba (about 17 minutes, IC ¥325). A combined day is doable: [teamLab in the morning](/en/travel-guide/teamlab-planets/), lunch at [Toyosu Senkyakubanrai](/en/travel-guide/toyosu-senkyakubanrai/), fountain in the evening.
- **SMALL WORLDS** in Ariake is one Rinkai Line stop (Kokusai-Tenjijo) from Tokyo Teleport — see [SMALL WORLDS Miniature Museum](/en/travel-guide/small-worlds/).
- **Asakusa by boat:** Tokyo Cruise water buses run between Odaiba Marine Park and Asakusa (direct boats ¥2,000, 50–65 minutes). It is a memorable way to link [Asakusa](/en/travel-guide/asakusa/) and the bay in one day.

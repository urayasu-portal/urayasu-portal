<p>Every autumn, hotels and shopping complexes across Urayasu and Maihama launch seasonal programs timed to Tokyo Disney Resort's "Disney Halloween." This guide covers <strong>how to enjoy Halloween 2026 in Urayasu and Maihama</strong>, organized around four kinds of visitor: people who want to wear costumes, people looking for a hotel, people chasing seasonal sweets, and people who are not going into the parks at all.</p>

<p>The section we put the most work into is <strong>where costumed visitors can actually change</strong>. There is no changing space inside the parks, and Disney explicitly asks guests not to change in restrooms, so planning ahead is essential. We compare the three options in the area by price, eligibility and how to book.</p>

<h2 id="basic">Disney Halloween 2026: the basics</h2>

<p>Tokyo Disney Resort's official site states that the special event "Disney Halloween" runs at Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea from <strong>September 16 (Wed) to October 31 (Sat), 2026</strong>. Special merchandise and menu items go on sale progressively from September 15 (Tue).</p>

<p>At Tokyo Disneyland, the parade "The Villains' Halloween: Into the Frenzy" featuring Disney Villains is performed once a day. At Tokyo DisneySea, alongside a harbor greeting and Duffy and Friends' "Autumn Surprise!", Lost River Delta hosts "Lazos de la Familia," themed on the film Coco, through November 2 (Mon).</p>

<p>For ticket types and how to buy them, see our <a href="/en/travel-guide/disney-tickets/">Tokyo Disney ticket guide</a>.</p>

<h2 id="costume">If you are wearing a costume: rules and where to change</h2>

<h3>There is no changing space in the parks</h3>

<p>Tokyo Disney Resort's official site puts it plainly.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>There are no changing spaces at Tokyo Disneyland or Tokyo DisneySea. Please change and apply make-up at home or at your hotel before you arrive.</p>
  <p>Please refrain from changing or applying make-up in public facilities, restrooms or multipurpose restrooms inside or outside the parks, or in lockers.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>In other words, <strong>"just change somewhere when I get there" is explicitly ruled out</strong>. That leaves three approaches: travel from home already in costume, change at your hotel, or reserve a dedicated changing facility. The table below covers that third option.</p>

<h3>Ikspiari Cinema Area 1F "Garden Site" (official)</h3>

<p>This is the only changing space Tokyo Disney Resort officially provides. It is paid, requires a reserved date and time, and cannot be booked or extended on the day.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Booths</strong>: 30 for women, plus 2 all-gender / accessible booths. Men use the all-gender / accessible booths</li>
  <li><strong>Occupancy</strong>: One person per booth. Junior high school students and older need their own booth. Each adult aged 18 or over may accompany up to two children of elementary school age or younger</li>
  <li><strong>Actual changing time</strong>: Slots are in 30-minute increments, but because reception, cleaning and turnover are included, the official guidance is <strong>about 25 minutes</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Reservations open</strong>: For October 16-31 slots, from <strong>12:00 on September 1 (Tue)</strong>. September slots opened on August 17</li>
  <li><strong>Cancellation</strong>: Until 23:59 seven days before use. After that, no cancellation and the full amount is charged. Reservations cannot be modified</li>
  <li>A MyDisney account (available to those aged 13 and over) and advance payment by credit card are required</li>
  <li>Cameras may not be used inside the changing space, and the parking service at Ikspiari does not apply</li>
</ul>

<p>Note that access to the Ikspiari building itself starts at different times: 7:00 in September and <strong>7:45 in October</strong>. Factor that in if you book an early slot.</p>

<h3>SPA &amp; HOTEL Maihama Eurasia "Halloween Costume-Change Plan 2026"</h3>

<p>A dedicated changing plan open to non-guests. The venue is the banquet room "Ostro" on the first floor of the new wing, and the plan <strong>includes one spa entry and luggage storage</strong> on top of the changing space.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Price</strong>: 4,000 yen on weekdays, 5,300 yen on weekends and holidays (tax included). September 21, 22 and 23 are charged at the weekend rate</li>
  <li><strong>Re-entry</strong>: You can come back and <strong>change as many times as you like</strong>, which is useful if you want a different look for day and night</li>
  <li><strong>Eligibility</strong>: One flat rate for ages 4 and over. Children aged 3 and under cannot use the facility</li>
  <li><strong>Hotel guest discount</strong>: Guests checking out of SPA &amp; HOTEL Maihama Eurasia or HOTEL Eurasia Maihama ANNEX pay 2,500 yen on weekdays and 3,800 yen on weekends and holidays</li>
  <li><strong>Booking</strong>: Through the ticketing site Gettii only. Reservations opened on August 3, 2026</li>
</ul>

<p>For the facility itself, see our <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/maihama-eurasia/">SPA &amp; HOTEL Maihama Eurasia hotel guide</a>, and for the hot spring, our <a href="/en/travel-guide/maihama-eurasia-spa/">Maihama Eurasia onsen guide</a>.</p>

<h3>Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay (hotel guests, women only, free)</h3>

<p>Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay in Akemi, Urayasu offers a <strong>free women-only changing room</strong> for hotel guests. It is open from 5:00 to 22:00, and notably it is <strong>free both before check-in and after check-out</strong>. The hotel also notes that with its free shuttle bus you can return and switch outfits between morning and afternoon.</p>

<p>The hotel additionally sells a <strong>limited-availability room plan with check-in from 7:00 a.m. and check-out at noon the following day</strong> (room only), designed for guests who want to get ready at the hotel before heading to the parks. The booking site only lets you select an estimated check-in time from 10:00 onwards, but the hotel states that arrival from 7:00 is in fact available.</p>

<p>A guest participation event is also planned, though no details had been announced as of August 30, 2026. See our <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/comfort-suites-tokyo-bay/">Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay hotel guide</a> for more.</p>

<h3>Costume rules worth knowing before you go</h3>

<p>These are the points from the official rules that are most worth checking in advance.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Eligible characters</strong>: Characters appearing in the parks or Disney hotels, performers from shows, and characters from Disney-distributed or Disney-streamed works on the official "eligible titles" list. The list explicitly names <strong>Spider-Man as not eligible</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Age</strong>: Guests of junior high school age and older may wear full costumes only during the designated full-costume periods</li>
  <li><strong>Face</strong>: Masks, false noses and rubber masks are not allowed, nor is make-up depicting blood or scars, whether drawn or applied as stickers. Accent make-up around the eyes or on the cheeks is fine</li>
  <li><strong>Props</strong>: Sticks and long staffs must be <strong>no taller than the wearer's waist</strong>. Bringing them in disassembled and putting them together inside the park is also prohibited</li>
  <li><strong>Items</strong>: Even as toys or replicas, firearms, blade-shaped weapons and armor are generally not permitted (toys purchased inside the parks are fine). Noise-making items, sports equipment and musical instruments are also not allowed</li>
  <li><strong>Length and width</strong>: Costumes or accessories that drag on the ground, or that are too wide to pass through the entrance, are not permitted</li>
  <li>These rules <strong>apply during photography too</strong>. Officially, a mask may be used for photos if it is simply held up in your hand</li>
</ul>

<p>Entry may take extra time while your costume is checked, and cast members may deny entry or ask you to leave. Tokyo Disney Resort states that whether something falls under the prohibited items is judged directly on site by cast members, and it does not answer individual enquiries in advance. If you are unsure about a costume, the safer choice is to avoid it.</p>

<h3>What to wear on October 1-15</h3>

<p>For the two weeks when full costumes are not allowed, the official wording draws this line.</p>

<ul>
  <li>✅ <strong>Allowed</strong>: An outfit that incorporates <strong>the colors or atmosphere</strong> of a particular character (what fans call Disneybound)</li>
  <li>❌ <strong>Not allowed</strong>: Using dresses, wigs or garments sold or made as costumes to <strong>reproduce or portray a specific character</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>There is no official "a T-shirt is fine" threshold. The distinction is whether you are recreating a character with costume garments and wigs, or working the colors and mood into clothing you could wear on an ordinary day.</p>

<h2 id="stay">Staying in the Halloween mood</h2>

<h3>Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel First Resort</h3>

<p>From September 15 (Tue) to October 31 (Sat), the hotel sells the "Halloween Spooky Night Stay," which puts you in a Halloween-decorated room. Rooms modeled on a "castle salon" are dressed to a "spooky fantasy" theme, and each comes with a <strong>witch's cape and hat you can use for photos</strong>.</p>

<p>Rates start at 16,700 yen per person for two sharing a room, 13,700 yen for three, and 11,525 yen for four, including breakfast and the right to purchase park tickets. See our <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/maihama-hotel-first-resort/">Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel First Resort guide</a> for more.</p>

<h3>Maihama View Hotel by HULIC</h3>

<p>From September 1 (Tue) to October 31 (Sat), the whole hotel runs "Maihama Halloween Stories," making it one of the most program-heavy hotels in the area.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Harvest Halloween Room</strong> (Sep 4 - Oct 31): A guest room decorated with scarecrows and pumpkins, blending an autumn harvest festival with Halloween. For 2-4 people per room</li>
  <li><strong>Trick or Treat!</strong> (Sep 4 - Oct 31): Children staying at the hotel find staff wearing pumpkin or ghost badges in the first-floor lobby and say the magic words to receive sweets. Free</li>
  <li><strong>Halloween Hollow Wall</strong> (Sep 1 - Oct 31): A large photo spot in the first-floor lobby</li>
</ul>

<p>The food side is covered under <a href="#sweets">Halloween sweets</a>. For the hotel itself, see our <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/maihama-view-hotel/">Maihama View Hotel by HULIC guide</a>.</p>

<h3>Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay</h3>

<p>As described above, the <strong>free women-only changing room</strong> and the <strong>7 a.m. check-in, noon-next-day check-out plan</strong> make this a practical base for anyone heading to the parks in costume. See <a href="#costume">the changing options comparison</a>.</p>

<h3>1955 Tokyo Bay by Hoshino Resorts (Shin-Urayasu)</h3>

<p>At 1955 Tokyo Bay in Hinode, Urayasu, the guests-only event "OLDIES HALLOWEEN NIGHT" runs from September 16 (Wed) to October 31 (Sat). No reservation is needed and there is no age restriction. Visit the house in the "2nd Room" lounge and say "Trick or Treat!" to receive a popcorn ball, and try seasonal desserts such as a pumpkin pie sundae.</p>

<p>The hotel is themed on 1950s America, so the atmosphere is quite different from the Maihama hotels. See our <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/hoshinoresorts-1955-tokyo-bay/">1955 Tokyo Bay guide</a>.</p>

<p>Disney Ambassador Hotel, Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta and Tokyo Disneyland Hotel serve Halloween special menus from September 15 to October 31. Offerings differ by hotel: the Chef Mickey lunch buffet (7,200 yen adults on weekdays, 7,500 yen on weekends and holidays), the Oceano buffet (7,500 / 8,000 yen) and the Sherwood Garden Restaurant buffet (6,800 / 7,300 yen), among others. See our <a href="/en/posts/disney-hotels-halloween-menu-2026/">Disney Hotels Halloween menu article</a>.</p>

{{< note >}}The hotel buffets and afternoon teas are open to visitors who are neither staying at the hotel nor entering the parks. Many require reservations, so book early.{{< /note >}}

<h2 id="outside">What you can enjoy outside the parks</h2>

<h3>Disney revival screenings at Cinema Ikspiari</h3>

<p>Cinema Ikspiari is screening three classic Disney titles in rotation from September 18 to November 26. The natural Halloween pick is <strong>Tim Burton's The Nightmare Before Christmas in Disney Digital 3D with Japanese dubbing (October 9 - November 5)</strong>. Coco and, new this year, the animated The Little Mermaid also screen.</p>

<p>Tickets for Coco and The Little Mermaid are 1,400 yen for adults and university students, 1,300 yen for seniors and 1,000 yen for high school students down to preschoolers. The Nightmare Before Christmas is in 3D, so 200 yen is added to each price when 3D glasses are included. No park ticket is required.</p>

<h3>Resort Line limited-design free pass</h3>

<p>From September 15 (Tue) to October 31 (Sat), the Disney Resort Line sells a <strong>"Disney Halloween" design free pass</strong> at ticket machines in every station, while stocks last. Two designs are available.</p>

<p>The price is the same as the standard one-day free pass, 700 yen for adults and 350 yen for children, with no premium for the seasonal design. Four Halloween souvenir medals are also on offer. On a day spent moving between the hotels and the parks from Maihama Station, the pass doubles as a keepsake.</p>

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Where can I change into my costume before going to the parks?</h3>
<p>There is no changing space inside the parks, and Disney asks guests not to change or apply make-up in restrooms or lockers inside or outside the parks. The options are the official paid changing space at Ikspiari Cinema Area 1F Garden Site (1,100-1,300 yen per 30-minute slot, advance reservation required), the costume-change plan at SPA &amp; HOTEL Maihama Eurasia (4,000 yen weekdays / 5,300 yen weekends and holidays, reservation only), and for hotel guests, the free women-only changing room at Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay. See <a href="#costume">the comparison</a>.</p>

<h3>Can I wear a full costume at any time during the Halloween season?</h3>
<p>No. Full costumes are permitted from September 15 to September 30 and from October 16 to October 31, and <strong>not from October 1 to October 15</strong>. The changing facilities are closed during that gap as well.</p>

<h3>What can I wear on October 1-15?</h3>
<p>Officially, an outfit that incorporates the colors or atmosphere of a character is fine for entry. What is asked against is using dresses, wigs or garments sold or made as costumes to reproduce or portray a specific character.</p>

<h3>Can men use the changing spaces?</h3>
<p>The official changing space has 30 women's booths plus 2 all-gender / accessible booths, and men use the latter. Because there are so few, book early. SPA &amp; HOTEL Maihama Eurasia has a dedicated men's changing booth. The changing room at Comfort Suites Tokyo Bay is women-only.</p>

<h3>Can I enjoy Halloween without a park ticket?</h3>
<p>Yes. Beyond the 3D screenings of The Nightmare Before Christmas at Cinema Ikspiari (October 9 - November 5), the hotel dessert buffets and afternoon teas are open to anyone.</p>

<h3>Are there stay plans with limited-edition Halloween merchandise?</h3>
<p>Five Official Hotels offer stay plans from September 16 to October 31 that include a Halloween-exclusive pouch with a handle. See <a href="#stay">Staying in the Halloween mood</a>.</p>

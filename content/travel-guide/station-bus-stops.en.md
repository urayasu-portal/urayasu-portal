Urayasu has three stations: **Urayasu Station** on the Tokyo Metro Tozai Line, and **Shin-Urayasu Station** and **Maihama Station** on the JR Keiyo Line. Every station has local bus stops, but each one labels its stops differently, and which direction leaves from where also differs by station. Pick the station you'll be using below to go to its guide.

## Choose Your Station {#stations}

- **[Urayasu Station bus stops](/en/travel-guide/urayasu-station-bus-stops/)** — Boarding stops A, B, D and E and the drop-off points of Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口). They are spread around the station, making this the easiest station to get lost at.
- **[Shin-Urayasu Station bus stops](/en/travel-guide/shin-urayasu-station-bus-stops/)** — South Exit bus terminal stops A–G, stop H along Symbol Road, and the North Exit, with airport buses at North Exit J. Stops for the Takasu, Hinode and Akemi areas, the Gyotoku and Moto-Yawata areas, and airport limousine buses.
- **[Maihama Station bus stops](/en/travel-guide/maihama-station-bus-stops/)** — South Exit bus rotary stops 01–04 and hotel shuttle stop C-3. Local buses toward Urayasu Station, Shin-Urayasu Station and Ichikawa Station.

## Station-to-Station Quick Reference {#between}

These are the combinations people use most, for example as an alternative when the Keiyo Line is suspended or to transfer between the Tozai Line and the Keiyo Line. Check each station's guide for the exact stop locations.

| From | To | Stop and route |
|---|---|---|
| Urayasu Station | Maihama Station | Urayasu-eki Iriguchi **E**, Route 9 (via Horie and Fujimi). Also Routes 4 and 12 from D, Route 6 from A, and Route 2 from B |
| Urayasu Station | Shin-Urayasu Station | Urayasu-eki Iriguchi **B**, Routes 2, 3, 11 and 18; Route 5 from E |
| Shin-Urayasu Station | Urayasu Station | **H**, Routes 3, 11 and 18; at the North Exit, Route 2 from 02 and Route 5 from 03 (as shown in the official search); Route 5 from South Exit A |
| Shin-Urayasu Station | Maihama Station | South Exit **B**, Route 14; North Exit Route 14 from 05 (as shown in the official search), and others |
| Maihama Station | Urayasu Station | **Stop 02**, Routes 9 and 6; Stop 03, Routes 2 and 4; Stop 04, Route 12. Route 4 buses bound for Tokyo Disneyland leave from Stop 04 |
| Maihama Station | Shin-Urayasu Station | **Stop 03**, Route 14. Routes 2 and 23 run via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi (新浦安駅北口) |

From Urayasu-eki Iriguchi to Maihama Station South Exit, Route 9 costs {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare" >}} for adults and takes about {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_minutes" >}} minutes. For fares and travel times on other sections, use the official search.

## What the Three Stations Have in Common {#common}

- **Bus operators**: Local buses in the city are mainly run by Keiseibus Chiba West (its Chidori Office was formerly Tokyo Bay City Kotsu). Keiseibus Tokyo routes also serve Shin-Urayasu Station.
- **Osampo Bus**: The city's community bus sometimes uses different stops from the local buses. For routes and fares, see [Urayasu City's official Osampo Bus page](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/todokede/machi/bus/1046220.html).
- **Airport limousine buses**: Shin-Urayasu is the only station in the city with airport buses leaving from in front of the station. From October 1, 2026, airport buses at Shin-Urayasu Station leave from North Exit J. In the Maihama area, check the official information for your bus, such as Stop 8 at Tokyo Disneyland for airport buses. Urayasu Station has no airport bus stop.
- **Hotel shuttle buses**: Free hotel shuttle buses run from Maihama Station and Shin-Urayasu Station. Pick-up points differ by hotel, so check your hotel's information.

{{< note >}}Bus stop assignments can change with timetable revisions. This guide reflects information as of October 1, 2026. Before you set out, search by stop name on the [official Keisei Bus Group search](https://transfer-cloud.navitime.biz/keiseibus-group/) to confirm the latest stop.{{< /note >}}

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Which station in Urayasu has the most confusing bus stops?

At Urayasu Station, the stops named Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口) are spread around the station. Shin-Urayasu Station is split between South Exit terminal stops A–G, stop H along Symbol Road, and the North Exit, and the same route can leave from different stops depending on its destination. At Maihama Station, local buses leave from South Exit stops 01–04.

### Which stop do I use to travel between stations by bus?

From Urayasu Station, Route 9 to Maihama Station leaves from E, and Routes 2, 3, 11 and 18 toward Shin-Urayasu Station leave from B. From Shin-Urayasu Station to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi, there are Routes 3, 11 and 18 from H, Routes 2 and 5 from the North Exit, and Route 5 from South Exit A. From Maihama Station to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi, Routes 9 and 6 leave from Stop 02, Routes 2 and 4 from Stop 03, and Route 12 from Stop 04. See each station's guide for details.

### Do bus stop assignments change?

They can change with timetable revisions or road works. At Shin-Urayasu Station, airport buses moved to North Exit J on October 1, 2026. Before you set out, check the latest stop locations and destinations on the official Keisei Bus Group or airport bus operator information.

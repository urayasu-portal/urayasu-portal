<h2 id="park">Going to the park in the rain</h2>

<p>The parks open rain or shine. Three things to know:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Outdoor shows and parades may be cancelled or changed.</strong> Check the official Tokyo Disney Resort app for same-day operations.</li>
  <li><strong>Indoor attractions, restaurants and shops run as usual.</strong> Rainy days can even mean shorter waits at outdoor headliners — plan around them and the day goes smoothly.</li>
  <li><strong>Rain gear is easy to buy locally.</strong> Park shops, Bon Voyage by Maihama Station, and convenience stores sell ponchos and umbrellas. Wear shoes you don't mind getting wet.</li>
</ul>

<p>If you haven't bought tickets yet, see the <a href="/en/travel-guide/disney-tickets/">ticket guide</a>. For getting there from your hotel, see the <a href="/en/travel-guide/hotels/shuttle/">shuttle bus guide</a> and <a href="/en/travel-guide/urayasu-taxi-airport-flat-rate-guide/">taxi guide</a>. Taxi demand spikes after park closing on rainy nights, so set up a ride-hailing app in advance.</p>

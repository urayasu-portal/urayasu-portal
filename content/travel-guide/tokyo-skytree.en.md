Of all the Tokyo landmarks, **Tokyo Skytree** is the one that comes to the Disney resort rather than the other way round: a **direct bus leaves from the Tokyo DisneySea and Tokyo Disneyland bus stops** for Tokyo Skytree Town, ¥1,000, no reservation, about 45–55 minutes — no Tokyo Station, no transfers, no stairs. At the other end you get the 634-metre tower with its two observation decks, the **Sumida Aquarium** that small children often prefer to the view, and **Tokyo Solamachi**, a 300-shop mall with a food floor and the baby rooms a family needs. It is also the Tokyo spot that pairs most naturally with [Asakusa](/en/travel-guide/asakusa/), a 20-minute walk across the river.

## Who is this for?

- Families who want **one high view of Tokyo** and would rather ride a bus than navigate subway transfers with a stroller.
- A **rainy or cold day**: deck, aquarium and mall are all indoors (the view is the only thing weather can spoil).
- An Asakusa day that needs an indoor second half.
- Anyone comparing Skytree with [SHIBUYA SKY](/en/travel-guide/shibuya-sky/): Skytree is higher, enclosed, cheaper for kids and bus-accessible; SHIBUYA SKY is open-air and more photogenic.

{{< note >}}
**Ticket prices changed in April 2026.** Skytree moved to **date-based (dynamic) pricing**: the old fixed weekday/holiday, advance/same-day and adult/teen/child tables are gone. Prices are shown per date on the official site; the age bands are now **adult 15+, child 6–14, under 6 free**. Older guides quoting ¥2,100 / ¥3,100 flat prices are out of date.
{{< /note >}}

## What to expect

### The observation decks

- **Tembo Deck (350 m):** three floors with a 360-degree view, a glass floor section, a café and a shop. On a clear winter day you see Mount Fuji; on an average day the sprawl to the horizon is the point.
- **Tembo Galleria (450 m):** a spiralling glass corridor climbing another 100 metres — the "walking in the sky" part. Bought as a combo or added on the day at machines on the deck (**¥1,400 adults / ¥700 children**).

**Hours (October 2026): 10:00–22:00 weekdays, 9:00–22:00 Saturdays and Sundays, last entry one hour before closing.** The sweet spots are right at opening (short lines, best visibility) and the hour before sunset (daylight, sunset and night view on one ticket, but the busiest slot — book that time in advance).

### Tickets in 2026

| | |
|---|---|

Prices rise on holidays and peak dates, so check your actual date. Children under 6 are free, which makes Skytree one of the cheaper landmarks for a family of two adults and two small kids.

### Sumida Aquarium (Solamachi 5F–6F)

A mid-size, beautifully lit aquarium: Magellanic penguins in an open pool you look down on, a jellyfish zone, a huge "Edo goldfish" gallery, and sea lions. **Open daily — weekdays 10:00–20:00, weekends and holidays 9:00–21:00, entry until one hour before closing. Adults ¥2,700, high school ¥2,000, elementary and junior high ¥1,400, children 3+ ¥900.** It is dim, quiet and stroller-friendly, and takes about 90 minutes — on a rainy day with a toddler it can be the main event, with the deck skipped entirely.

### Tokyo Solamachi — the mall underneath

**All floors 10:00–21:00; restaurant floors (6F–7F and 30F–31F) 11:00–23:00.** A large food court and a basement food hall solve lunch; the 30th–31st floor restaurants have a free-ish view (you pay for the meal). Family facilities: **baby rooms with changing tables, nursing rooms, hot water and microwaves on 1F, 3F and 4F**; the tower itself has stroller parking on 4F, limited stroller rental, lockers on 4F and nursing rooms on the deck. Character shops (including a large Pokémon Center and Ghibli, Kirby Café by reservation) are here too — a smaller sibling of [Tokyo Character Street](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-character-street/).

## How to get there from the Tokyo Disney Resort area

**Direct bus (easiest from the parks and the Maihama hotels):** the **Skytree Shuttle** (Tobu Bus / Keisei Bus) runs between **Tokyo Skytree Town (stop No. 2) – Kinshicho Station – (Kasai Station on some runs) – Tokyo DisneySea – Tokyo Disneyland**. **Adults ¥1,000, children ¥500**; 16 departures from the parks (TDS from 9:40 to 21:25; TDL from 9:50 to 21:35) and 17 toward the parks (from Skytree Town 6:50–19:50). About **45 minutes to DisneySea, 55 to Disneyland**. No reservations — first come, and standing is not allowed, so for a sunset return line up 15 minutes early. Cash, Suica/PASMO and contactless credit cards are accepted on board.

**By train from Urayasu Station (Tozai Line):** Tozai to **Kudanshita**, Hanzomon Line to **Oshiage** (the station under the tower) — **about 45–50 minutes, IC ¥252**, one transfer. Faster but pricier: Tozai to Nihombashi, Toei Asakusa Line to Oshiage — about 36 minutes, ¥359.

**By train from Maihama:** Keiyo Line to Hatchobori, Hibiya Line to Ningyocho, Toei Asakusa Line to Oshiage — about 45 minutes, roughly ¥540 (estimate). Honestly, take the bus.

At **Oshiage Station**, follow the signs for Tokyo Skytree Town; the tower entrance is on the 4th floor, about 7 minutes from the gates by escalator and elevator.

Suica/PASMO works on all of the above; contactless credit cards work on Tokyo Metro, Toei and the bus, but not on the JR Keiyo Line.

## A plan that uses the bus both ways

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:50 | Skytree Shuttle from the Tokyo Disneyland bus stop (or 9:40 from DisneySea) |
| 10:45 | Arrive Skytree Town; Sumida Aquarium first (quiet in the morning) |
| 12:30 | Lunch at Solamachi's food floor |
| 14:00 | Tembo Deck with a pre-booked time (or walk to Asakusa, 20 minutes, and come back for sunset) |
| 16:30 | Solamachi shops; snack |
| 17:30 | Bus back to the parks or hotels, arriving around 18:30 |

For sunset on the deck, book the slot one hour before sunset and take a later bus (last departures 19:50 toward the parks — after that, train).

## With kids, and when it rains

- **Strollers:** allowed up to the deck (elevators); stroller parking on 4F if you prefer; limited rental.
- **Queues:** with a timed online ticket the entry wait is short; the counter line on weekends is the one to avoid (and it costs ¥500 more per ticket).
- **Rain or fog:** the deck is enclosed, but a cloudy day means no view — check the live camera on the official site before buying a same-day ticket, and fall back on the aquarium and mall.
- **Heights:** the glass floor is small and optional.

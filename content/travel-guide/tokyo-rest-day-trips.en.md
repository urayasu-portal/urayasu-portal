Two park days in a row is the point at which most families based in Maihama or Urayasu start asking what else is nearby. The answer is more than the guidebooks suggest — **Tokyo Bay's attractions are 25–35 minutes away with one transfer, and central Tokyo is 35–50 minutes** — but the right choice depends less on fame than on what you need that day: legs that need a rest, kids who need to run, a craving for "real Japan", or the big-city views. This page sorts **14 spots** by exactly that, flags which are **indoor, free or stroller-hostile**, and gives four ready-made days. Every spot links to a full guide with hours, prices and the route from Maihama, Shin-Urayasu and Urayasu Station.

## Which day are you having?

- **Everyone is tired →** [Relax & recharge](#cat-relax): parks, gardens, footbaths, the onsen.
- **The kids have energy, the parents don't →** [Family fun](#cat-family): indoor museums and character shops where the children lead.
- **You came to Japan, not just to Disney →** [Experience Japan](#cat-japan): temple, market, sumo, kimono.
- **You want to see Tokyo →** [Explore Tokyo](#cat-explore): the bay, the towers, the crossing.

{{< note >}}
**Two local rules before you pick.** (1) From Maihama and Shin-Urayasu, anything on the bay (Odaiba, Toyosu, Ariake, Kasai) is reached via **Shin-Kiba**, two stops away — never via Tokyo Station. (2) For central Tokyo, the **Tozai Line from Urayasu Station** usually beats the Keiyo Line on time and price; a bus or taxi from Maihama to Urayasu Station takes about 20 minutes. The [routes and fares cheat sheet](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-sightseeing-access/) has the numbers.

## Four ready-made days

### The rainy rest day (fully indoor, bay side)

Morning at [SMALL WORLDS](/en/travel-guide/small-worlds/) (Keiyo → Shin-Kiba → Rinkai Line, 25 min) → lunch at the museum café or Ariake Garden → one Rinkai stop to Tokyo Teleport for LEGOLAND Discovery Center in [DECKS, Odaiba](/en/travel-guide/odaiba/) → home via Shin-Kiba. Swap SMALL WORLDS for [teamLab Planets](/en/travel-guide/teamlab-planets/) if you booked it, and finish at [Toyosu Senkyakubanrai](/en/travel-guide/toyosu-senkyakubanrai/) instead.

### The "real Japan" day (from Urayasu Station)

[Asakusa](/en/travel-guide/asakusa/) at 9:00 before the crowds (Tozai → Ginza Line, 35 min, ¥252) → kimono or tea ceremony if booked → lunch → walk 20 minutes over the river to [Tokyo Skytree](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-skytree/) for the aquarium or the deck → **Skytree Shuttle bus straight back to the parks** (¥1,000). With older kids, replace Skytree with [Ryogoku's sumo and Edo-Tokyo Museum](/en/travel-guide/ryogoku-sumo/).

### The bay day that ends with lights

Market lunch at [Toyosu Senkyakubanrai](/en/travel-guide/toyosu-senkyakubanrai/) (via Shin-Kiba and the Yurikamome, 30 min) → free rooftop footbath → Yurikamome across to [Odaiba](/en/travel-guide/odaiba/) → beach and malls → **TOKYO AQUA SYMPHONY** fountain show at 18:45 or 19:30 → Rinkai Line to Shin-Kiba, home by 21:00. Nothing booked, almost nothing paid.

### The quiet day (parents' choice)

Coffee and [Kiyosumi Garden](/en/travel-guide/kiyosumi-garden/) (Tozai → Oedo, 20 min from Urayasu Station) → Fukagawa Edo Museum with the kids or MOT without → early lunch → back by 14:00 for a nap → evening soak at [Maihama Eurasia](/en/travel-guide/maihama-eurasia-spa/). If even 20 minutes on a train is too much, [Kasai Rinkai Park](/en/travel-guide/kasai-rinkai-park/) is one stop from Maihama and free.

## Three things that go wrong

1. **Buying the ticket too late.** SHIBUYA SKY sells out two weeks ahead at 00:00; teamLab weekends sell out; Skytree counter tickets cost ¥500 more than online. Book these the week you book flights — see the [pre-trip checklist](/en/travel-guide/pre-trip-checklist/).
2. **Tokyo Station.** The Keiyo Line platforms are a 15–20-minute walk from everything else in the station. Only go that way for [Tokyo Character Street](/en/travel-guide/tokyo-character-street/) (which is on the Keiyo side) or the Yamanote Line.
3. **Wrong day of the week.** Tsukiji is shut on Sundays, holidays and most Wednesdays; the Sumo Museum is weekdays only; the Edo-Tokyo Museum, MOT and Hachitama close on Mondays. Match the spot to the day before you decide.

Shin-Urayasu Station on the JR Keiyo Line has a **bus terminal at the South Exit**, with buses to the Takasu, Hinode and Akemi areas of the city and to the Gyotoku and Moto-Yawata areas. Buses bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口) are split between **H on the South Exit side**, the **North Exit** and other stops, and each route uses a different stop. This guide is designed so you can **find the right stop from your destination**.

**What this guide covers**

- Routes and destinations from South Exit bus terminal stops A–G and stop H along Symbol Road
- The differences between H, the North Exit and South Exit A for buses to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi
- Stops for the Osampo Bus, airport limousine buses and the Cemetery Park shuttle

## Overview of the Stops {#map}

The South Exit bus terminal has stops **A–G** in front of the station. **H** is outside the terminal, along Symbol Road on the South Exit side. Most local buses are run by **Keiseibus Chiba West, Chidori Office (formerly Tokyo Bay City Kotsu)**. Buses to the Gyotoku and Moto-Yawata areas are run by Keiseibus Chiba West's Shiohama Office, and 瑞75 is a Keiseibus Tokyo route.

## Urayasu Station (H, North Exit, South Exit A) {#urayasu}

**Routes 3, 11 and 18 bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi leave from H.** H is outside the South Exit bus terminal, along Symbol Road. If you board at the North Exit, **Route 2 leaves from 02 and Route 5 from 03** in the official search. Route 5 also runs from South Exit A to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi.

**Route 1 from South Exit A** is the Kitazakae loop and goes via Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi. On the timetable, **"医療" means buses toward Tokyo Bay Medical Center, and "浦" means buses terminating at Urayasu-eki Iriguchi**. Choose the bus that suits your destination, including unmarked buses.

The drop-off point for buses bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi and the Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi stop served by Route 1 are separate stops. The locations on the Urayasu Station side are covered in the [Urayasu Station bus stop guide](/en/travel-guide/urayasu-station-bus-stops/).

## Maihama Station (South Exit B, North Exit 05, and others) {#maihama}

**Route 14 from South Exit B** (Benten-Tomioka Line) goes to Maihama Station. Route 14 also runs from the North Exit, shown as **05** in the official search. Some buses terminate at Chidori Shako, so check the destination display.

Route 23 to Maihama Station also leaves from H, and Route 2 to Maihama Station from the North Exit. Check Route 23's operating days and times on the official timetable.

The Osampo Bus Maihama Line also links Shin-Urayasu Station and Maihama Station, but it winds through public facilities and residential areas in the city, so a one-way trip takes about 60 minutes. If you're in a hurry, compare Keiyo Line and local bus times.

## Hinode, Akemi & Sogo Koen (South Exit C & D) {#hinode}

For the Hinode area, take Routes 16 and 17 (Hinode Line) from **South Exit C**; for Akemi and Sogo Koen, take Routes 3 and 23 (Urayasu Higashi Danchi Line) from **South Exit D**. Route 11 (Symbol Road Line), which runs along Symbol Road to Bay Park, Sogo Koen and Hinode-minami, also leaves from D.

Route 11 to Hinode-minami is a year-round route that goes to the nearest stop for Urayasu City Cemetery Park. Route 17 has late-night buses to Bay City Urayasu, with a different fare from usual. Check times on the official timetable.

## Takasu & Takasu Kaihin Koen (South Exit E & F) {#takasu}

Buses to the Takasu area are split between **South Exit E** and **South Exit F**. E serves Route 15 (潮音の街 Line) and Route 18 (Akemi-Takasu Line); F serves Route 10 (Takasu Line) and Route 19 (Takasu-minami Line). **Route 10 terminates at Minato-minami (Tekko Danchi)**, and **Route 19 at Takasu Kaihin Koen**. Routes 15 and 18 also differ in their stops on the way and final destinations, so check the official timetable for a bus that stops where you want to go.

## Gyotoku, Moto-Yawata & Minami-Gyotoku {#gyotoku}

Route 浦安03 from **South Exit G** is run by Keiseibus Chiba West's Shiohama Office. It goes via High Town Shiohama, with some buses terminating at Gyotoku Station and others going to Moto-Yawata-eki Minamiguchi.

**Route 瑞75** is a Keiseibus Tokyo route that goes via Minami-Gyotoku Station to **Edogawa Sports Land**. Before boarding, check the location on [the official timetable and bus stop map for 瑞75](https://transfer-cloud.navitime.biz/keiseibus-group/courses?busstop=00020224).

## Osampo Bus Stop {#osanpo}

All three routes of the city's community bus, the Osampo Bus, start from Shin-Urayasu Station. According to the Urayasu City Cultural Hall's official directions, exit the ticket gates and, with the bus rotary on your right, go down the slope on the left and walk straight toward AEON Shin-Urayasu; the Osampo Bus stop is on your left. The Maihama Line also has a "Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi" stop. For routes and fares, see [Urayasu City's official Osampo Bus page](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/todokede/machi/bus/1046220.html).

## Airport Limousine Buses (North Exit J) {#airport}

Limousine buses run by Tokyo Airport Transport and others go from Shin-Urayasu Station to Haneda Airport and Narita Airport.

{{< warn >}}**From the first bus on October 1, 2026, airport buses leave from Shin-Urayasu Station North Exit J.** The stop moved from the former H along Symbol Road. It's a new bus stop along the road in front of the North Exit, on the Urayasu Brighton Hotel Tokyo Bay side. Check the location on [Tokyo Airport Transport's relocation notice and map](https://www.limousinebus.co.jp/ja/information/detail/c8j66a6xjrkwb8izdoe2v9gz/).{{< /warn >}}

On the relocation map, the stopping positions for local buses, the Osampo Bus and airport buses are shown separately. Local bus stop H is still listed in the official list, but don't confuse it with the waiting area for airport buses.

The adult fare to Haneda Airport is {{< fact "limousine.haneda_fare" >}}. Check whether you need a reservation, and the timetable, on [Tokyo Airport Transport's route information](https://www.limousinebus.co.jp/ja/line/detail/Haneda-Shiurayasu/).

## Free Cemetery Park Shuttle Bus (Obon & Higan) {#shuttle}

At Obon and Higan, the city sometimes runs free temporary shuttle buses linking Urayasu Station and Shin-Urayasu Station with the Cemetery Park. At Obon in 2026, the Shin-Urayasu Station pick-up point was **near stop 31 of the Osampo Bus Maihama Line at the North Exit**, and the drop-off point on the return trip was in a different place. Pick-up points and times may change each time the service runs, so check Urayasu City's official website.

{{< note >}}Bus stop assignments can change with timetable revisions. This guide reflects information as of October 1, 2026. Before you set out, check the latest stop on the [official Keisei Bus Group search](https://transfer-cloud.navitime.biz/keiseibus-group/).{{< /note >}}

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where are the bus stops at Shin-Urayasu Station?

They are split between stops A–G at the South Exit bus terminal, stop H along Symbol Road on the South Exit side, and the North Exit. From October 1, 2026, limousine buses to Haneda and Narita airports leave from North Exit J.

### Which stop do I use from Shin-Urayasu Station to Urayasu Station?

Routes 3, 11 and 18 bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口) leave from H. At the North Exit, Route 2 leaves from 02 and Route 5 from 03 in the official search, and Route 5 also leaves from South Exit A. Route 1 from A is the Kitazakae loop via Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi (浦安駅東口), with some buses via the medical center and some terminating at Urayasu-eki Iriguchi. Check the notes on the destination.

### Which stop do I use from Shin-Urayasu Station to Maihama Station?

Options include Route 14 from South Exit B and Route 14 from North Exit 05 in the official search. Route 23 to Maihama Station also leaves from H, but check its operating days and times. The Osampo Bus Maihama Line winds through the city and takes about 60 minutes.

### Where do I catch the limousine bus from Shin-Urayasu Station to Haneda or Narita Airport?

From the first bus on October 1, 2026, it leaves from Shin-Urayasu Station North Exit J. The stop moved from H on Symbol Road to a new bus stop at the North Exit. Check the location on the map in Tokyo Airport Transport's relocation notice.

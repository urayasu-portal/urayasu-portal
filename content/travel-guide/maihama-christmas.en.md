In winter, Urayasu and Maihama fill with Christmas spirit to match Tokyo Disney Resort's "Disney Christmas," from the hotels to the station plazas and cultural venues. This guide covers how to enjoy Christmas 2026 in Urayasu and Maihama, organized around five kinds of visitor: people going to the parks, people who want to order a Christmas cake, people looking for a festive meal, people looking for a hotel, and people who are not going into the parks at all.

Hotel Christmas cake orders are already open. Order deadlines and pick-up periods vary widely from hotel to hotel, so start with the comparison table.

## Which one are you?

- Enjoying Christmas in the parks: check the dates, shows and fireworks, plus the short-hours days with no evening entertainment
- Ordering a hotel Christmas cake: check the comparison table of order deadlines and pick-up periods
- Spending the evening at a dinner or buffet: many events run only on certain days, so check the dates first
- Staying somewhere festive: compare plans with limited-edition merchandise and Christmas-decorated rooms
- Enjoying Christmas without entering the parks: make the most of free illuminations, photo spots and concerts

### Ticket prices

Park tickets use variable pricing. On the official calendar, a 1-Day Passport (adult) in December costs 10,900 yen on weekdays, 11,900 yen on Sundays and 12,400 yen on Saturdays; Thursday, December 24 is 11,900 yen and Friday, December 25 is 12,400 yen. Ticket types and where to buy them are explained in our [Tokyo Disney ticket guide](/en/travel-guide/disney-tickets/).

### Costume rules

Full costumes for guests aged junior high school and older are allowed only during the full-costume periods of Disney Halloween, so they are not allowed during the Christmas season. Guests are asked not to wear dresses, wigs or costume garments that reproduce a specific character. Everyday outfits inspired by a character's colors or atmosphere are fine.

### Duffy & Friends winter program

From Monday, November 2, 2026 to Wednesday, January 13, 2027, Tokyo DisneySea sells winter-themed Duffy & Friends merchandise and food souvenirs. Some Christmas items are sold only until December 25. Details are on the Tokyo Disney Resort official website.

### Christmas at the Disney hotels

All Disney hotels put up Christmas decorations and illuminations from November 11. Special Christmas menus are offered at the following three hotels from November 1 to December 25.

| Hotel | Main restaurants |
| --- | --- |
| [Disney Ambassador Hotel](/en/travel-guide/hotels/ambassador-hotel/) | Chef Mickey, Empire Grill, Tick Tock Diner, Hyperion Lounge |

| [Tokyo Disneyland Hotel](/en/travel-guide/hotels/tdl-hotel/) | Sherwood Garden Restaurant, Canna, Dreamers Lounge |

At some restaurants, the course content and price change between the period up to December 18 and the period from December 19. Fantasy Springs Hotel and Toy Story Hotel have decorations only, with no special Christmas menus.

### Disney Resort Line

From Wednesday, November 11 to Friday, December 25, 2026, the "Disney Christmas Liner," a train with a Christmas-themed interior, will be in service.

Only one train, the purple one, runs as the Christmas Liner. Inside, you'll find characters such as Mickey Mouse and Minnie Mouse along with Christmas decorations, hand straps decorated to look like Santa hats, and Christmas songs.

The operating schedule will be announced on the official website from Sunday, November 8. If you want to ride it, check the [official Disney Resort Line page (Japanese)](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/tdr/resortline/) before you go.

#### Christmas sticker

From Wednesday, November 11 to Friday, December 25, Disney Resort Line riders can receive an original Christmas sticker.

One sticker per person. To get one, ask a nearby cast member.

#### Fan stamp rally with 7 stamps

From Tuesday, November 10 to Friday, December 25, the "Disney Christmas 2026" fan stamp rally takes place.

Collect all 7 stamps placed at the 4 stations to complete a Christmas artwork featuring Mickey Mouse, a snowman and a teddy bear.

| Station | Stamp stands |
| --- | --- |
| Resort Gateway Station | 2 |
| Tokyo Disneyland Station | 1 |
| Bayside Station | 2 |
| Tokyo DisneySea Station | 2 |

To take part, you need a Free Ticket that is valid that day and has already been used to start your trip. Show it to a cast member at a station to receive one stamp card per ticket.

Every station has stamp stands, so you can start from any of them. To cover all 4 stations in one day, using a Free Ticket to ride around is the easiest way.

#### Two Christmas-design Free Tickets

From Tuesday, November 10 to Friday, December 25, Christmas-design Free Tickets are sold at the ticket machines at each station.

In 2026 there are two designs: one featuring Mickey Mouse and friends, and one featuring the character リルリンリン.

They cost the same as a regular Free Ticket: the 1-day ticket is 700 yen for adults and 350 yen for children. The limited designs end when sold out.

#### Four souvenir medals

From Sunday, November 1 to Friday, December 25, four Christmas-design souvenir medals are available.

Each station sells one design, so you can collect them by visiting all 4 stations. Each medal costs 100 yen.

{{< note >}}The Resort Line's Christmas events start on different dates. Souvenir medals start on November 1, Free Tickets and the stamp rally on November 10, and the Christmas Liner and sticker giveaway on November 11.{{< /note >}}

### Shin-Urayasu Station illumination (free)

From Friday, November 6, 2026 to Saturday, February 27, 2027, the 16 zelkova trees in the plaza in front of Shin-Urayasu Station are lit with about 30,000 lights, as part of Urayasu City's arts program "Shin-Ura Art Square."

On November 6, a lighting ceremony starts at 16:30. After a dance show, the lights are switched on at around 17:05, followed by a Christmas mini-concert (times may shift). Admission is free and no registration is needed.

### Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay "Santa Claus's Room"

From November 1 to December 25, the "Santa Claus's Room" photo spot appears in the hotel's 1F lobby. Props such as Santa hats are provided, and it is free for everyone, including non-staying visitors.

The hotel also lights up its "Precious Time Illumination" from sunset to midnight between November 1, 2026 and February 28, 2027, illuminating the "Candle Gate" at the entrance, the dome at the main entrance and the fountain in the courtyard.

### The Christmas tree at Ikspiari

At Ikspiari in Maihama, a Christmas tree made from a real fir tree returns to Celebration Plaza in the center of the complex. The building is decorated with trees, wreaths and illuminations, and shops and restaurants offer Christmas gifts and limited-time menus. Lighting dates and other details are announced on Ikspiari's official website.

Cinema Ikspiari, inside the complex, is scheduled to open "Ebenezer: A Christmas Carol" on Friday, November 13.

## FAQ

### Until when can I order a hotel Christmas cake in Urayasu and Maihama?

Deadlines differ by hotel; the earliest is Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay on December 12. Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay and First Resort take orders until December 15, and Maihama View Hotel and Grand Nikko until December 22. Cakes are limited in number, so order early.

### Can I enjoy Christmas without a park ticket?

Yes. Options include the Shin-Urayasu Station illumination, the "Santa Claus's Room" at Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay, the Christmas tree at Ikspiari, hotel buffets and afternoon teas, and the Christmas concert at Urayasu Civic Hall.

### Can I wear a costume to Tokyo Disney Resort during the Christmas season?

Full costumes for guests aged junior high school and older are allowed only during the Halloween full-costume periods, so not during the Christmas season. Everyday outfits inspired by a character's colors or atmosphere are fine.

### Are there days during the Christmas season with no evening fireworks or parade?

Yes. Tokyo DisneySea on Friday, November 27, and Tokyo Disneyland on Friday, December 4, close at 18:30, and the evening fireworks and parade are not held.

### Is there a New Year's Eve countdown at Tokyo Disney Resort?

No. "New Year's Eve" operations will not be held on December 31, 2026.

### Is Hilton Tokyo Bay included in the Christmas stay plans with limited-edition merchandise?

Yes. For Christmas, six Official Hotels including Hilton Tokyo Bay are included.

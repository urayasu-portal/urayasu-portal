Buses can't enter the station-front rotary at Urayasu Station on the Tozai Line. As a result, bus stops all sharing the same name, Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口), are scattered around the station in **seven locations**, and first-time visitors often wonder where they're supposed to queue. This guide is designed so you can **find the right stop from your destination**.

**What this guide covers**

- Where stops A, B, D and E are, and which routes and destinations leave from each
- How to get to Maihama Station and Disney, Shin-Urayasu Station, and the Takasu and Hinode areas
- Stops for the Osampo Bus, the Cemetery Park shuttle and late-night buses
- The city's announced plan to consolidate Stop E

## Overview of the Stops {#map}

All local buses are run by **Keiseibus Chiba West, Chidori Office** (formerly Tokyo Bay City Kotsu). There are four boarding stops, A, B, D and E, divided by direction.

| Stop | Approximate location | Routes | Main destinations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | North side of Yanagi-dori (やなぎ通り), east of Stop B | 6 | Maihama Station via Shiyakusho-mae (市役所前) and Tokai-dai Urayasu Koko Iriguchi (東海大浦安高校入口) |
| **B** | North side of Yanagi-dori, west of Stop A | 2, 3, 11, 18 | 2: Maihama Station via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi (新浦安駅北口); 3: Sogo Koen (総合公園) via Shin-Urayasu Station; 11: Sogo Koen and Hinode-minami (日の出南); 18: Takasu Kaihin Koen (高洲海浜公園) |
| **D** | South side of Yanagi-dori, in front of Urayasu Station No. 4 Bicycle Parking | 4, 12 | Maihama Station, Tokyo Disneyland (東京ディズニーランド) and Chidori Shako (千鳥車庫) (4); Maihama Station via Tokyo DisneySea (東京ディズニーシー) and the resort hotels (12) |
| **E** | On Miyamae-dori (宮前通り), south of the intersection with Yanagi-dori | 5, 9 | Shin-Urayasu Station (5); Maihama Station and Keisei Rose Town (京成ローズタウン) (9) |

In addition, there is the **Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi (浦安駅東口)** bus stop (Route 1) on the east side of the station, and the **Osampo Bus** stop "Urayasu-eki (浦安駅)" near the station. Both are explained in the [Osampo Bus and Route 1](#osanpo) section.

Keisei's official boarding information uses the letters **A, B, D and E**. The city's layout map marks the drop-off points as **1, 2 and 3**. There's no need to look for a "C" as a name for the drop-off points. You can check the locations on [Keisei's official bus stop map](https://transfer-cloud.navitime.biz/keiseibus-group/courses?busstop=00020739#pole-map), and the difference between boarding and drop-off points on [the city's layout map (Town Planning Newsletter No. 5)](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/047/342/05.pdf).

{{< warn >}}Besides the boarding stops, Urayasu-eki Iriguchi also has **drop-off-only points**. If you wait for your next bus where you got off, it won't stop there. To board, always move to one of stops A, B, D or E.{{< /warn >}}

## Maihama Station & Disney (Routes 2, 4, 6, 9, 12) {#maihama}

Buses from Urayasu-eki Iriguchi to Maihama Station are **Routes 2, 4, 6, 9 and 12**, split across the four stops A, B, D and E. **Route 9**, via Horie and Fujimi, costs {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare" >}} for adults and takes about {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_minutes" >}} minutes. Travel time varies by service and road conditions.

| Route | Stop | Via | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **9** Maihama Line | E | Horie 6-chome (堀江六丁目), Fujimi 3-chome (富士見三丁目) | To Maihama Station. Some buses terminate at Keisei Rose Town |
| **4** Tomioka Line | D | Shiyakusho Iriguchi (市役所入口), Yubinkyoku-mae (郵便局前) | Some buses go to "Tokyo Disneyland". Starts at Urayasu-eki Iriguchi |
| **12** Maihama Resort Line | D | Tokyo DisneySea, resort hotels | To Maihama Station. Check operating days and times on the official timetable |
| **6** City Hall Line | A | Shiyakusho-mae, Tokai-dai Urayasu Koko Iriguchi | Stops at City Hall and the Cultural Hall before Maihama Station |
| **2** Imagawa Line | B | Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi, Juntendo Byoin-mae (順天堂病院前) | To Maihama Station via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi |

### How to Get to Stop D

Stop D is **on the south side of Yanagi-dori, in front of Urayasu Station No. 4 Bicycle Parking**. From the station side, you'll need to cross Yanagi-dori. Check the route on the official bus stop map above, and use the pedestrian bridge or a crosswalk.

### How to Get to Stop E

Stop E is **on Miyamae-dori, south of the intersection with Yanagi-dori**. It's on a different street from A, B and D on Yanagi-dori, so check its location on the official bus stop map before heading there. The city is working on consolidating Stop E ([see below](#future)).

Route 2 (Imagawa Line), which runs to Maihama Station via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi, also leaves from Stop B, but the fare is {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare_via_shin" >}}. It takes a longer way around than Route 9 via Horie and Fujimi. Route 37 (Daisankaku Line) from Minami-Gyotoku Station **does not stop at Urayasu-eki Iriguchi**, so it isn't included in the boarding information from Urayasu Station.

## Shin-Urayasu Station (Routes 1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 18) {#shinurayasu}

Buses to Shin-Urayasu Station are concentrated at **Stop B**. Every bus that comes to B passes through Shin-Urayasu Station, whatever its final destination, so if you just need to get to Shin-Urayasu Station, queuing at B is the safe choice.

| Route | Stop | Via | Final destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2** Imagawa Line | B | Shinmei-ura (神明裏), Shobo Honbu-mae (消防本部前) | Maihama Station via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi |
| **3** Urayasu Higashi Danchi Line | B | Shin-Urayasu Station, Akemi 5-chome (明海五丁目) | Sogo Koen |
| **11** Symbol Road Line | B | Shin-Urayasu Station, Bay Park (ベイパーク) | Sogo Koen, Hinode-minami |
| **18** Akemi-Takasu Line | B | Shin-Urayasu Station, Takasu-bashi (高洲橋) | Takasu Kaihin Koen |
| **5** Horie Line | E | Horie 6-chome, Higashino Chuo (東野中央) | Shin-Urayasu Station |
| **1** Kitazakae Line | Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi (shown as 02 in the official information) | Kitazakae 2-chome (北栄二丁目), Mihama Chugakko (美浜中学校). Some buses go via Tokyo Bay Medical Center | Shin-Urayasu Station (check the destination and stops on the way) |

If you have business in the Horie or Higashino area, Route 5 from Stop E is convenient.

## Takasu, Hinode & Sogo Koen (Routes 3, 11, 18) {#takasu}

Buses beyond Shin-Urayasu Station to the Takasu, Hinode and Akemi areas also leave from **Stop B**. Route 3 goes to Sogo Koen via Akemi 5-chome, Route 11 goes to Sogo Koen and Hinode-minami via Symbol Road and Bay Park, and Route 18 goes to Takasu Kaihin Koen via Takasu-bashi. Check the official timetable for the routes and services that stop at your destination.

Route 11 to Hinode-minami is a year-round route that goes to the nearest stop for Urayasu City Cemetery Park (浦安市墓地公園). It's separate from the free shuttle bus that runs at Obon and Higan, so you can use it to visit graves at any time of year.

## Osampo Bus and Route 1 (Tokyo Bay Medical Center) {#osanpo}

### Osampo Bus "Urayasu-eki" Stop

The Medical Center Line of the city's community bus, the Osampo Bus, links Tokyo Bay Urayasu Ichikawa Medical Center with Shin-Urayasu Station, stopping at Urayasu Station on the way. Its stop is in a **different place** from the local buses' Urayasu-eki Iriguchi. According to the Cultural Hall's official directions, go from the ticket gates under the elevated tracks, turn left without crossing the crosswalk, and cross the scramble intersection toward the Sukiya restaurant. Walk past the restaurant and, without crossing at the first crosswalk, keep left to reach the stop for buses to Shin-Urayasu Station. For the stop for buses to Tokyo Bay Medical Center, check the city's bus stop map. The fare is a flat ¥100. For route and fare details, see [Urayasu City's official Osampo Bus page](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/todokede/machi/bus/1046220.html).

### Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi Stop (Route 1, Kitazakae Line)

Route 1 boards at **Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi**, and the official timetable shows the stop as **02**. Buses to Shin-Urayasu Station run either via Kitazakae 2-chome and Mihama Chugakko or via Tokyo Bay Medical Center. If you're going to the medical center, choose a bus marked "医療" (medical) on the timetable. Some buses are bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi, so check the destination too. This is a separate stop from Urayasu-eki Iriguchi A, B, D and E.

## Getting Off the Bus and Walking to the Station {#alight}

Buses bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi use drop-off points separate from the boarding stops. The city's layout map shows **three drop-off points, 1, 2 and 3**: 1 is near A and B on the south side of Yanagi-dori, 2 is west of D on the south side of Yanagi-dori, and 3 is roughly opposite D on the north side of Yanagi-dori. Depending on where you get off, use the pedestrian bridge or a crosswalk and follow the signs for Urayasu Station.

When returning from Maihama Station to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi, take **Route 9 bound for Urayasu-eki Iriguchi**. The stops on the Maihama Station side are covered in the [Maihama Station bus stop guide](/en/travel-guide/maihama-station-bus-stops/). Route 6 also goes to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi via City Hall. Route 37 to Minami-Gyotoku Station does not pass through Urayasu-eki Iriguchi.

## Free Cemetery Park Shuttle Bus (Obon & Higan) {#shuttle}

At Obon and Higan, the city sometimes runs free temporary shuttle buses linking Urayasu Station and Shin-Urayasu Station with the Cemetery Park. At Obon in 2026, the Urayasu Station pick-up point was **in front of Sakazen (サカゼン)**, and the drop-off point on the return trip was in a different place. Pick-up points and times may change each time the service runs, so check the latest information on Urayasu City's official website.

## Late-Night Buses {#night}

Route 9 has late-night buses, which leave from Stop E. Late-night buses don't go all the way to Maihama Station; they terminate on the way at **Keisei Rose Town**, and the fare is double the regular fare. Check times on the operator's timetable.

## Planned Consolidation of Stop E {#future}

In the Urayasu Station Area Town Planning Newsletter No. 5 (December 2025), Urayasu City announced a plan to first consolidate Stop E on Miyamae-dori onto **city-owned land in the block south of Yanagi-dori**. For the timing of the move and specific construction details, check the city's latest information.

{{< note >}}Bus stop assignments can change with timetable revisions. This guide reflects information as of September 2026. Before you set out, check the latest layout on Keiseibus Chiba West's stop map.{{< /note >}}

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### How many bus stops are there at Urayasu Station?

According to the city's layout map, Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口) has four boarding stops (A, B, D and E) and three drop-off points (1, 2 and 3), for a total of seven. Keisei's official boarding information also lists A, B, D and E. Separately, there is Route 1 from Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi (浦安駅東口) and the Osampo Bus stop "Urayasu-eki (浦安駅)".

### Which stop do I use from Urayasu Station to Maihama Station or Tokyo Disneyland?

Route 9 via Horie and Fujimi leaves from E, Route 6 via City Hall from A, and Route 2 via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi from B. Routes 4 and 12 leave from D: Route 4 includes buses to Tokyo Disneyland via Maihama Station, and Route 12 includes buses to Maihama Station via Tokyo DisneySea and the resort hotels. Route 9 costs {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare" >}} for adults and takes about {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_minutes" >}} minutes. Check destinations and operating days on the official timetable.

### Where is the Urayasu-eki Iriguchi E bus stop?

It is on Miyamae-dori, south of the intersection with Yanagi-dori. Route 5 (to Shin-Urayasu Station) and Route 9 (to Maihama Station and Keisei Rose Town) leave from here. Before you set out, check its location on Keisei's official bus stop map.

### Which stop do I use from Urayasu Station to Shin-Urayasu Station?

From B, Route 2 goes via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi, and Routes 3, 11 and 18 go via Shin-Urayasu Station. Route 5 from E also goes to Shin-Urayasu Station. Route 1 leaves from Urayasu-eki Higashiguchi, shown as "02" in the official information. The Osampo Bus Medical Center Line also has buses to Shin-Urayasu Station, from a different stop than the local buses.

### Is there a limousine bus from Urayasu Station to Haneda Airport?

There is no limousine bus stop around Urayasu Station. To get to the airport, take a limousine bus from Shin-Urayasu Station, or take the Tozai Line to Nihombashi and transfer there. Check the stop at Shin-Urayasu Station on the airport bus operator's latest information.

### Will the bus stop locations change in the future?

Urayasu City has announced a plan to first consolidate Stop E on Miyamae-dori onto city-owned land in the block south of Yanagi-dori. For the timing of the move and any operational changes, check the latest information from the city and the operator.

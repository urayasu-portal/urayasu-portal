Maihama Station on the JR Keiyo Line is the gateway to Tokyo Disney Resort. Local buses and hotel shuttle buses are gathered at the **South Exit bus rotary**, but the numbered local bus stops and the hotel shuttle "C" stops are separate. Airport limousine buses don't leave from the station front; they leave from the **Tokyo Disneyland bus terminal**. This guide is designed so you can **find the right stop from your destination**.

**What this guide covers**

- Routes and destinations from South Exit stops 01–04
- Where C-3 is, used by Hiyori Hotel Maihama and other hotels
- Stops for airport limousine buses and the Osampo Bus

## Overview of the Stops {#map}

Local buses are run by **Keiseibus Chiba West**. Routes to the Chidori area and around the city are run by the **Chidori Office (formerly Tokyo Bay City Kotsu)**, and Route 市舞01 by the Shiohama Office. There are four stops, 01 to 04, shown with two digits such as "01" and "02" in the official stop list.

| Stop | Routes | Main destinations |
|---|---|---|
| **01** | 20, 市舞01 | Toward Chidori Shako (千鳥車庫) and Clean Center (クリーンセンター) (20); Ichikawa-eki Minamiguchi (市川駅南口) (市舞01) |
| **02** | 6, 9, 37 | Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口) (9, 6); Minami-Gyotoku-eki (南行徳駅) (37) |
| **03** | 2, 4, 14, 23 | Urayasu-eki Iriguchi via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi (新浦安駅北口) (2); Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (4); Shin-Urayasu-eki (新浦安駅) (14); Sogo Koen (総合公園) (23) |
| **04** | 4, 12, 25, 37 | Tokyo Disneyland (東京ディズニーランド) (4); Urayasu-eki Iriguchi via the resort hotels (12); Sogo Koen and Takasu Kaihin Koen (高洲海浜公園) (25); Tokyo DisneySea (東京ディズニーシー) (37) |
| **C-3** | Hotel shuttles | Shuttle buses for Hiyori Hotel Maihama, Henn na Hotel Maihama Tokyo Bay and others |

You can check locations on the bus stop map in [Keisei Bus Group's Maihama Station stop list](https://transfer-cloud.navitime.biz/keiseibus-group/courses?busstop=00020617).

## Urayasu Station (Stops 02, 03, 04) {#urayasu}

To get to Urayasu Station, **Route 9 from Stop 02** runs via Fujimi and Horie to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi for {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare" >}} (adults), taking about {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_minutes" >}} minutes. Stop 02 is at the South Exit rotary. Check its location on the official bus stop map above.

| Route | Stop | Via | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **9** Maihama Line | 02 | Fujimi 3-chome (富士見三丁目), Horie 6-chome (堀江六丁目) | To Urayasu-eki Iriguchi. Some buses go to Tokai Daigaku Urayasu Koko Iriguchi (東海大学浦安高校入口), so check the destination display |
| **6** City Hall Line | 02 | Tokai-dai Urayasu Koko Iriguchi (東海大浦安高校入口), Shiyakusho-mae (市役所前) | Stops at City Hall and the Cultural Hall before Urayasu-eki Iriguchi |
| **4** Tomioka Line | 03 | Yubinkyoku-mae (郵便局前), Shiyakusho Iriguchi (市役所入口) | To Urayasu-eki Iriguchi |
| **2** Imagawa Line | 03 | Juntendo Byoin-mae (順天堂病院前), Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi | To Urayasu-eki Iriguchi via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi. Fare {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare_via_shin" >}} |
| **12** Maihama Resort Line | 04 | Resort hotels, Shiyakusho Iriguchi | To Urayasu-eki Iriguchi. Check operating days and times on the official timetable |

**Route 4 uses different stops depending on its destination.** Buses to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi leave from Stop 03, and buses to Tokyo Disneyland from Stop 04. Route 37 is similar: buses to Minami-Gyotoku Station leave from Stop 02 and buses to Tokyo DisneySea from Stop 04, so always check the stop number and the destination together.

When you arrive at Urayasu Station, you get off at the Urayasu-eki Iriguchi drop-off point. The stops on the Urayasu Station side are covered in the [Urayasu Station bus stop guide](/en/travel-guide/urayasu-station-bus-stops/). Route 37 (Daisankaku Line) also leaves from Stop 02, but it goes to Minami-Gyotoku Station and does not pass through Urayasu-eki Iriguchi.

## Shin-Urayasu Station (Stop 03) {#shinurayasu}

To get to Shin-Urayasu Station, take **Route 14 from Stop 03** (Benten-Tomioka Line). Route 2 from Stop 03 also passes Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi. Route 23 (Urayasu Higashi Danchi Line) goes to Sogo Koen via **Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi**.

The Osampo Bus Maihama Line also links Maihama Station and Shin-Urayasu Station, but it winds through the city, so a one-way trip takes about 60 minutes. The stops on the Shin-Urayasu Station side are covered in the [Shin-Urayasu Station bus stop guide](/en/travel-guide/shin-urayasu-station-bus-stops/).

## Ichikawa Station, Chidori & Minami-Gyotoku (Stops 01 & 02) {#ichikawa}

From **Stop 01**, Route 20 (Chidori Line) runs toward Chidori Shako and Clean Center in the Chidori area, and Route 市舞01 runs to Ichikawa-eki Minamiguchi. Route 市舞01 is a non-stop express with no stops on the way, running only on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays (except during Obon and the New Year holidays). Check operating days on the official timetable.

For Minami-Gyotoku Station, take Route 37 (Daisankaku Line) from **Stop 02**.

## Hotel Shuttle Buses (C-3) {#hotel}

The South Exit bus rotary at Maihama Station has **"C" stops** where hotel shuttle buses pull in. Leave the South Exit ticket gates, go down the stairs on the right at the end, and you'll find the taxi stand; follow the road and you'll reach the hotel bus stops. **Hotels such as Hiyori Hotel Maihama and Henn na Hotel Maihama Tokyo Bay use the area around C-3.** Each hotel's stopping position isn't necessarily the same.

C-3 sometimes has no bus stop sign. Pick-up points and times differ by hotel, so check your hotel's shuttle information and be at the stop before the departure time. For hotels inside Tokyo Disney Resort, use the Disney Resort Line monorail or the hotels' own buses.

## Airport Limousine Buses & Highway Buses {#airport}

Limousine buses to Haneda and Narita airports don't leave from local bus stops 01–04 at Maihama Station. According to official information, buses from Tokyo Disneyland to the airports leave from **bus terminal Stop 8**. It's a walk from Maihama Station toward Tokyo Disneyland. Highway bus terminals and stop numbers vary by destination, so check [Tokyo Disney Resort's guide by destination](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/tdr/access/bus/tdl_return.html).

Check stop numbers and times on the official websites of airport bus operators such as Tokyo Airport Transport. Among station fronts in Urayasu, Shin-Urayasu Station is the one with direct buses to the airports.

## Osampo Bus Stop {#osanpo}

The Maihama Line of the city's community bus, the Osampo Bus, links Maihama Station and Shin-Urayasu Station, stopping at City Hall and other public facilities. The stop is **on the Maihama Station South Exit side**. On [the city's Maihama Line stop map](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/046/220/02_mai_map_20241012.pdf), it's shown as **"1 舞浜駅" (1 Maihama-eki)**. This "1" is the Osampo Bus stop number, so don't confuse it with local bus "Stop 01"; check the location on the map. For routes and fares, see [Urayasu City's official Osampo Bus page](https://www.city.urayasu.lg.jp/todokede/machi/bus/1046220.html).

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Where are the bus stops at Maihama Station?

Local buses leave from stops 01–04 at the South Exit rotary. Hotel shuttles use the C stops, and hotels such as Hiyori Hotel Maihama and Henn na Hotel Maihama Tokyo Bay use the area around C-3. Airport buses don't leave from the station's local bus stops; they leave from Stop 8 at the Tokyo Disneyland bus terminal.

### Which stop do I use from Maihama Station to Urayasu Station?

Route 9 via Fujimi and Horie leaves from Stop 02, costs {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_fare" >}} for adults and takes about {{< fact "local_bus.urayasu_maihama_minutes" >}} minutes. Route 6 from Stop 02, Routes 2 and 4 from Stop 03, and Route 12 from Stop 04 also go to Urayasu-eki Iriguchi (浦安駅入口). Route 4 buses bound for Tokyo Disneyland leave from Stop 04, so check the destination display.

### Which stop do I use from Maihama Station to Shin-Urayasu Station?

Route 14 from Stop 03 goes to Shin-Urayasu Station, and Routes 2 and 23 go via Shin-Urayasu-eki Kitaguchi (新浦安駅北口). The Osampo Bus Maihama Line winds through the city and takes about 60 minutes.

### Where do I catch a free hotel shuttle bus?

Some hotels use the C stops at Maihama Station South Exit. Hotels such as Hiyori Hotel Maihama and Henn na Hotel Maihama Tokyo Bay use the area around C-3. Stopping positions, times and conditions of use differ by hotel, so check your hotel's official shuttle information.

### Where do I catch the limousine bus from Maihama Station to Haneda or Narita Airport?

Airport buses don't leave from local bus stops 01–04 at Maihama Station. According to official information, buses from Tokyo Disneyland to the airports leave from bus terminal Stop 8. Check stops and times on the official Tokyo Disney Resort and airport bus operator websites.
